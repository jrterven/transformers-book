## Chapter 22. Image and video understanding

- [Figure 22.3](ch22/ch22_pretrained_patch_projection.png) and
  [Figure 22.4](ch22/ch22_pretrained_patch_similarity.png) use NASA's
  portrait of Eileen Collins. The experiment resizes and translates the
  photo and adds patch outlines and heatmaps. The photograph is in the
  public domain. [Source and credit](https://scikit-image.org/docs/stable/api/skimage.data.html#skimage.data.astronaut).
- [Figure 22.8](ch22/ch22_pretrained_fan_frames.png) samples frames from
  *Collections in Motion: Reversible Fan*, Cooper Hewitt, Smithsonian Design
  Museum. The panels show the sampled frames in both directions and mark
  the model's approximate crop. [Video source](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Collections_in_Motion_Reversible_Fan.webm) ·
  [CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).

## Chapter 25. Speech and audio

[Figure 25.3](ch25/ch25_parakeet_frontend.jpg) and
[Figures 25.7–25.10](INDEX.md#chapter-25-speech-and-audio) visualize
recordings or measurements from the chapter's audio experiment. The voice
is from Google FLEURS, Latin American Spanish test example 1880. The rain
and dog recordings are *Louisiana Rain 1.wav* by uberhuberman and
*rose_bark.wav* by nfrae, using the ESC-10 files. The figure inputs were
adjusted in gain, resampled where needed, and mixed for the noisy conditions.
[FLEURS (CC BY 4.0)](https://huggingface.co/datasets/google/fleurs) ·
[ESC-10 (CC BY 3.0)](https://github.com/karolpiczak/ESC-50/tree/33c8ce9eb2cf0b1c2f8bcf322eb349b6be34dbb6) ·
[Recording and processing credits](../examples/ch25_speech_audio/README.md#listen-to-the-model-inputs).

## Data and simulation figures

- Chapter 28's forecasting figures (28.6–28.9) use data from the U.S. Energy
  Information Administration's EIA-930 balance file for July–December 2026,
  snapshot of November 13, 2026. [Source](https://www.eia.gov/electricity/930-content/about.html).
- Chapter 29's tabular figures (29.6–29.9) use UCI's Raisin and Concrete
  Compressive Strength datasets. The underlying data are
  [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
  [Dataset citations](../examples/ch29_tabular/README.md).
- Chapter 30's protein figures (30.3 and 30.7–30.9) use deposited protein
  sequences and coordinates from the Protein Data Bank. The underlying data
  are CC0. [wwPDB usage policy](https://www.wwpdb.org/about/usage-policies) ·
  [Structure and depositor credits](../examples/ch30_protein/README.md#data-and-weights).
- [Figure 31.7](ch31/ch31_smolvla_frames.pdf) shows camera views rendered
  during the book's SmolVLA rollout. The scene uses the LIBERO benchmark
  and LeRobot's LIBERO assets. [LIBERO](https://github.com/Lifelong-Robot-Learning/LIBERO) ·
  [LeRobot assets](https://huggingface.co/datasets/lerobot/libero-assets).
