# Figures from *Transformers*

The book's figures are organized by chapter and appendix. Look up a figure
by its number in the November 2026 edition in the [figure index](INDEX.md).
PNG, JPG, and PDF files retain the quality and format used in the book.
Diagrams drawn in LaTeX are available as standalone vector PDFs.

To download a figure, open its file and choose **Download raw file**.
To get the full collection, [download the repository ZIP](https://github.com/jrterven/transformers-book/archive/refs/heads/main.zip)
and open its `figures/` folder.

You may use the original book figures in class slides and handouts. A sample
credit is *Juan Terven and Diana Córdova, Transformers, Figure 4.1*.
Keep the credit and license details for figures with third-party material.
[Credits and reuse](CREDITS.md).
